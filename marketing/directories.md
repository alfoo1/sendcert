## Core fields

**Name:** SendCert

**Website:** https://www.sendcert.org

**Tagline (60 chars max):**
Free certificates by email, each with a verification link

**Short description (≈160 chars):**
Create personalized PDF certificates and email one to each recipient in minutes.
Every certificate gets a verification page and one-click Add to LinkedIn. Free.

**Long description:**
SendCert is a free web tool for teachers, trainers, course creators and
nonprofits to create and send certificates in a few clicks. Pick one of six
templates or upload your own design, add your recipients from a spreadsheet or
by typing them, and every person gets their own PDF certificate by email.

Each certificate comes with a public verification page and a unique ID, so
employers, schools and licensing boards can check it's genuine. Recipients can
add it to the Licenses & certifications section of their LinkedIn profile in
one click.

No sign-up needed: send up to 100 certificates a month as a guest, or 300 with
a free account. We never store recipient email addresses from guest sends.

**Key features:**
- Six certificate templates, or upload your own design (PNG/JPG)
- Bulk send from CSV, Excel or a pasted list
- One personalized PDF per recipient, delivered by email
- Verification page with a unique certificate ID for every certificate
- One-click "Add to LinkedIn profile" for recipients
- Free, no sign-up; reply-to address so recipients can answer you

**Pricing:** Free. 100 certificates/month without an account, 300/month with a
free account. No credit card.

**Platform:** Web (any browser). **License:** Free / proprietary.

**Categories / tags:** certificate generator, certificate maker, digital
certificates, certificate verification, credential management, e-learning,
education, training, event management, nonprofit

**Alternatives to (check each exists in the directory before linking):**
Certifier, Accredible, Sertifier, Canva (certificate maker), Certopus,
Virtualbadge.io, Autocrat (Google Workspace add-on)
