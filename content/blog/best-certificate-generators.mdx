The best certificate generator depends on what you need after the design. If you want to email a personalized PDF to every participant for free, a bulk sender like SendCert or Certify'em is enough. If you need digital badges, analytics and an API, look at a credentialing platform like Certifier, Accredible or Credly. If you only need a nice design, Canva works, but you'll deliver the certificates yourself.

Below are nine tools we looked at, grouped by what they're built for. Prices and limits change often, so we only list what each tool publishes and link to its own pricing page for the rest. Information checked in November 2026.

## Simple bulk senders

### SendCert

[SendCert](/) is a free tool for sending certificates in a few clicks. Pick one of six templates or upload your own design, add your recipients from a CSV, an Excel file or a pasted list, and each person gets a personalized PDF by email. Every certificate gets its own verification page with a unique ID, and recipients can add it to their LinkedIn profile in one click.

- **Best for:** teachers, trainers, nonprofits and event organizers who want certificates out the same day.
- **Free option:** 100 certificates a month without an account, 300 with a free account. No credit card.
- **Limits:** no digital badges, no LMS integrations or API yet, and recipient names need to be in a Latin alphabet. The interface is in English, though your certificates and emails can be in any language that uses one.

Full disclosure: SendCert is our product. If you run trainings for an organization, see [SendCert for organizations](/for-organizations).

### Certify'em

[Certify'em](https://www.certifyem.com) is a Google Forms add-on. It sends a certificate automatically when someone submits a form or passes a quiz, which makes it handy for teachers who already run quizzes in Google Forms.

- **Best for:** classrooms and online quizzes built in Google Forms.
- **Free option:** up to 60 recipients a day, with a "Made for free by Certify'em" footer. Paid plans raise the daily limit, remove the footer and, on the top plan, add QR-code verification.
- **Limits:** it lives inside Google Workspace, and the design options are narrower than a dedicated tool.

### Autocrat

Autocrat is a free Google Sheets add-on for mail merge. It fills a Google Docs or Slides template with each row of your sheet, can export each one as a PDF, and can email it.

- **Best for:** people comfortable building their own workflow in Google Workspace.
- **Free option:** free.
- **Limits:** you set up merge tags, templates and email settings yourself. There's no verification page or LinkedIn option, and Gmail's daily sending limits apply.

## Certificate and credential platforms

### Certifier

[Certifier](https://certifier.io) is a full platform for digital certificates and badges, with bulk issuing, verification pages, LinkedIn sharing, analytics and integrations.

- **Best for:** organizations that issue credentials regularly and want analytics and integrations.
- **Free option:** a free plan with up to 250 credentials a year. Paid plans are monthly; see [Certifier's pricing](https://certifier.io/pricing).
- **Limits:** you'll outgrow the free plan quickly if you run several cohorts a year.

### Accredible

[Accredible](https://www.accredible.com) is a credentialing platform used by certification bodies, universities and large training programs. It issues certificates and badges with hosted verification and integrates with learning platforms.

- **Best for:** formal certification programs that need integrations, branding and analytics at scale.
- **Free option:** no public free plan. Plans are priced by number of recipients; see [Accredible's pricing](https://www.accredible.com/pricing).
- **Limits:** more than a workshop or a single course needs.

### Credly

[Credly](https://info.credly.com) is a digital badge network used by large companies and certification programs. Earners collect badges in a Credly profile and share them.

- **Best for:** enterprise badge programs with a dedicated budget.
- **Free option:** none; pricing is by custom quote.
- **Limits:** built around badges rather than PDF certificates, and priced for large organizations.

### Sertifier

[Sertifier](https://sertifier.com) issues digital certificates and badges for institutions, with verification and sharing options.

- **Best for:** institutions that want certificates and badges in one platform.
- **Free option:** a limited free option; check their pricing page for the current limits.
- **Limits:** paid plans are needed for regular volume.

### Certopus

[Certopus](https://certopus.com) lets you design, generate, deliver and verify certificates from one place.

- **Best for:** small teams that want a certificate platform with a low entry price.
- **Free option:** a limited free plan; check their pricing page for the current credential allowance.
- **Limits:** most features beyond the basics are on paid plans.

## Design tools

### Canva

[Canva](https://www.canva.com) has hundreds of certificate templates and is the easiest place to design one. With Bulk Create, available on paid and education plans, you can generate a certificate for every row of a spreadsheet.

- **Best for:** designing the certificate itself.
- **Free option:** free for design.
- **Limits:** Canva doesn't email each certificate to its recipient and has no verification page. A common setup is to design in Canva, export a PNG, and [send it with a bulk sender](/blog/how-to-send-100-certificates-at-once). SendCert accepts your Canva design as the template.

## How to choose

- **You need certificates out today, for free:** SendCert, or Certify'em if everything already happens in Google Forms.
- **You run quizzes in Google Forms:** Certify'em.
- **You like building your own workflow in Google Sheets:** Autocrat.
- **You need badges, analytics, integrations or an API:** Certifier, Accredible or Sertifier.
- **You run an enterprise badge program:** Credly or Accredible.
- **You only need the design:** Canva, then a bulk sender to deliver it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free certificate generator?

For sending certificates by email, SendCert (100 a month without an account, 300 with a free one) and Certify'em (60 recipients a day on the free plan) are the most generous free options. Certifier's free plan covers 250 credentials a year. For design only, Canva is free.

### Which certificate tools include a verification page?

SendCert, Certifier, Accredible, Credly, Sertifier and Certopus all give each certificate a page where anyone can confirm it's genuine. Certify'em adds QR-code verification on its top paid plan.

### Can I design in Canva and send with another tool?

Yes. Export your Canva design as a PNG or JPG with a blank space for the name, upload it to a bulk sender like SendCert, and every recipient gets a personalized PDF by email.

### Do I need a credential platform for a workshop or a course?

Usually not. If you need a professional PDF with a way to verify it, a free bulk sender is enough. Credential platforms make sense when you issue badges, need integrations or run a formal certification program.
